I study how knowledge, technology and networks shape innovation and growth.

I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor of Economic Policy at the Department of Economics and Statistics “Cognetti de Martiis”, University of Torino, and a Collegio Carlo Alberto Affiliate. I am a fellow at BRICK, Collegio Carlo Alberto, and a deputy member of the Steering and Scientific Committee of HighEst Lab for Big Data Analytics and Artificial Intelligence.

My research looks at the determinants and effects of knowledge, technology and networks on innovation and growth, and at how they can help territories and firms navigate the green and digital transitions. It has appeared in Research Policy, Economic Geography, Economics of Innovation and New Technology and Eurasian Business Review.

I hold a PhD in Economics from the University of Torino and Collegio Carlo Alberto. I have been a visiting researcher at the EU Joint Research Centre, ESSEC Business School, INGENIO (CSIC-UPV) and University College Dublin.

Research areas

  • Innovation & knowledge

    Technological knowledge, innovation dynamics, firm capabilities, and the economic effects of new technologies.

  • Green & circular economy

    Environmental technologies, circular-economy knowledge, green diversification, and sustainable transitions.

  • Economic geography

    Regional development, spatial knowledge flows, technological diversification, and economic resilience.

  • Network analysis

    Collaboration networks, embodied knowledge flows, and the green and digital transformation of firms and regions.
