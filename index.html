Economics of innovation · Network analysis

I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor of Economic Policy at the Department of Economics and Statistics “Cognetti de Martiis”, University of Torino, and a Collegio Carlo Alberto Affiliate.

I am a fellow at BRICK, Collegio Carlo Alberto, and a deputy member of the Steering and Scientific Committee of HighEst Lab for Big Data Analytics and Artificial Intelligence.

I hold a PhD in Economics from the University of Torino and Collegio Carlo Alberto. I have been a visiting researcher at the EU Joint Research Centre, ESSEC Business School, INGENIO (CSIC-UPV) and University College Dublin.

Research

What I work on

I study the determinants and effects of knowledge, technology and networks on innovation and growth, and how they can help territories and firms navigate the green and digital transitions. My work connects technological knowledge with firms, regions and policy, using patent data, network analysis and spatial methods.
